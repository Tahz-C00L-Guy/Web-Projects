1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website or a third-party service saves on your device. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, do the same job. This Cookie Policy explains what Star Wrought Iron (L.L.C.) (النجمة للحديد المشغول (ش.ذ.م.م)), a limited liability company licensed by Dubai Economy & Tourism (Trade Licence No. 301117; Commercial Register No. 77587) (“we”) uses on swi.ae and how you control it. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

2. In short

  • By default we use strictly necessary storage only — no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies.
  • The only non-essential item is the Google Maps embed on our contact section. It stays blocked until you allow it.
  • You can accept, reject, or change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer.

We do not use analytics, advertising, social-media tracking pixels or profiling cookies. Our typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which does not set cookies but does receive your IP address and browser details when a page opens. The image host (Cloudinary) and our web host (Vercel) likewise receive your IP address when they send you a file — see the Privacy Policy. Links to Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp are ordinary links; those services set their own cookies only after you leave our Website.

4. Google Maps

Until you consent, the contact section shows a placeholder instead of the map, and your browser makes no request to Google. If you click “Accept non-essential” in the banner or “Load map” on the placeholder, the map appears and Google’s cookies may be set. A “Get directions” link to Google Maps is always available; it opens Google in a new tab and only sends data when you click it. Google’s use of data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).

5. How to manage your choice

  • On this Website: select here or in the footer, then choose Accept or Reject. Rejecting removes the map and keeps it blocked.
  • In your browser: you can delete cookies and site data or block cookies in your browser settings. Clearing this site’s data also clears your saved choice, and the banner will appear again.
  • Rejecting non-essential cookies does not affect how you can use the rest of the Website or contact us.

6. Changes and contact

If we add any new non-essential cookie or third-party service, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first. Questions? Contact Star Wrought Iron (L.L.C.)
