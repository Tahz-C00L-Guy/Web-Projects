Our Heritage

A Family Legacy
of Ironcraft

Founded in 1990 in the UAE, Star Wrought Iron has spent over three decades mastering the ancient art of wrought iron fabrication. What began as a family workshop has grown into a respected name in bespoke metalwork — serving residential, commercial and heritage restoration projects across the UAE.

"Every piece carries the weight of tradition and the precision of modern craftsmanship — shaped and finished by skilled hands, built to endure."

Our artisans bring an intimate knowledge of iron's character to every commission. From grand estate gates to delicate interior screens, skilled hands are involved at every stage.

Quality & Safety

Safety Standards & Policy

Every project is executed under strict workshop and site safety protocols.

PPE Compliance
All workshop personnel are equipped with certified protective gear — welding masks, gloves and safety boots — for every fabrication task.
Site Risk Assessment
Pre-installation site surveys identify and mitigate on-site hazards before any work begins.
Quality Control Checks
Multi-stage inspection of welds, finishes and structural integrity before every piece leaves the workshop.
Trained Workforce
Artisans and installers are regularly trained on UAE occupational health & safety practice.
Fire & Equipment Safety
Workshop machinery is routinely serviced, with fire suppression and safety equipment on hand at all times.
Licensed Operations
Operating under Dubai Economy & Tourism trade licence no. 301117 and applicable UAE regulations.
