Our Heritage

A Family Legacy
of Ironcraft

Founded in 1990 in the UAE, Star Wrought Iron has spent over three decades mastering the ancient art of wrought iron fabrication. What began as a family workshop has grown into the Northern Emirates' most trusted name in bespoke metalwork — serving luxury residential estates, landmark commercial developments, and heritage restoration projects across the region.

"Every piece carries the weight of tradition and the precision of modern craftsmanship — hand-shaped, hand-finished, built to endure."

Our artisans — many of whom have worked with the family for over two decades — bring an intimate knowledge of iron's character to every commission. From grand estate gates to delicate interior screens, the hand is always present.

Quality & Safety

Safety Standards & Policy

Every project is executed under strict workshop and site safety protocols.

PPE Compliance
All workshop personnel are equipped with certified protective gear — welding masks, gloves and safety boots — for every fabrication task.
Site Risk Assessment
Pre-installation site surveys identify and mitigate on-site hazards before any work begins.
Quality Control Checks
Multi-stage inspection of welds, finishes and structural integrity before every piece leaves the workshop.
Trained Workforce
Artisans and installers are regularly trained on UAE occupational health & safety practice.
Fire & Equipment Safety
Workshop machinery is routinely serviced, with fire suppression and safety equipment on hand at all times.
Insured & Compliant
Fully licensed operations, compliant with UAE municipality and civil defence requirements.
