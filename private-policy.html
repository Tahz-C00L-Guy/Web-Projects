1. Who we are

We are the “controller” of your personal data under Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (the “PDPL”) and its executive regulations, as they apply from time to time.

3. Consent and withdrawing it

Where we rely on your consent, you give it freely by ticking the box on the enquiry form, by choosing “Accept non-essential” or “Load map” in the cookie banner, or by contacting us directly. You may withdraw consent at any time — for the enquiry form by contacting us using the details in section 1; for cookies through the “Cookie settings” link in the Website footer. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it, or processing we are required by law to continue (for example, keeping invoices).

5. Transfers outside the UAE

Formspree, Vercel, Cloudinary and Google process data on servers outside the UAE, including in the United States. We use these providers only for the purposes above. Where your data is transferred outside the UAE we do so in line with the PDPL, using contractual safeguards and the providers’ compliance measures, and we only transfer the minimum information needed for the service. By ticking the consent box on the enquiry form you acknowledge that your enquiry will be handled by Formspree outside the UAE. If you prefer that your data not leave the UAE, contact us by telephone or WhatsApp instead of using the form.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a contract: up to 12 months from our last contact with you, then deleted.
  • Customer, project, invoice and payment records: for the length of the contract and afterwards for at least five years, or for longer if UAE law, a warranty claim or a dispute requires it.
  • Formspree copies: Formspree keeps submissions in our account for as long as needed to manage the project or respond to follow-up queries, and we delete older submissions once they are no longer required.
  • Server logs (Vercel): kept for the short period set by the host.
  • Cookie choice: stored in your browser for up to 12 months, after which we ask again.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to the Website, limiting access to enquiries to the staff who need them, and choosing established service providers. No system is completely secure. If a personal-data breach occurs that is likely to harm you, we will notify you and the competent authority as the PDPL requires.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right, subject to the conditions and exceptions in the law, to:

  • be informed about, and obtain a copy of, the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data or complete incomplete data;
  • ask us to delete your data, or to restrict or stop processing it;
  • ask to receive your data in a structured, commonly used format and, where possible, have it transferred to another controller (portability);
  • object to processing, and to decisions based solely on automated processing (we make none); and
  • withdraw consent at any time (see section 3).

To use these rights, contact us using the details in section 1. We may ask you to confirm your identity and will respond within the time set by the PDPL. If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the UAE Data Office or another competent authority.

9. Children

The Website and our services are directed at adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

The Website itself sets no cookies. It stores one item in your browser (your cookie choice). The Google Maps embed may set cookies, but only if you allow non-essential cookies. Full details and how to change your choice are in our Cookie Policy.

11. Testimonials, project photographs and client names

The Website shows client testimonials with the client’s name and general location, and photographs of completed projects. We publish a client’s name or testimonial only with their permission, which we ask for in writing (for example by email or WhatsApp). We try not to publish images that show identifiable people or a private address. If you appear in, or own a property shown in, a photograph or testimonial and would like it changed or removed, contact us and we will act promptly. We keep written permission on file for each named testimonial and any logo or client name shown.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows the current version. If we make a significant change to how we use personal data, we will highlight it on the Website.
